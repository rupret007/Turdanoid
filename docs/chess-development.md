## Decisions and research

- [Chess.com bot options](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8614091-how-can-i-play-against-the-chess-com-bots) provide color choice and optional hints/takebacks. [Official takeback guidance](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8594400-can-i-take-back-a-bad-move) treats these as learning aids. We choose untimed computer practice and pass-and-play, manual hints and clearly described undo. Difficulty labels describe search settings, not calibrated Elo.
- [Lichess features](https://lichess.org/features) include learning, analysis and PGN export. We choose a compact readable move list, review controls and PGN export rather than a separate chess platform. These are design choices, not evidence of retention.
- Recent first-person feedback: [September 2026 history controls](https://www.reddit.com/r/lichess/comments/1wa2eaq/in_lichess_app_no_forward_or_back_button_when/) and [July 2026 weak bot behavior](https://www.reddit.com/r/chessbeginners/comments/1us1fq5/the_new_beginner_bots_on_chesscom_are_seemingly/). These anecdotes favor visible navigation and useful opponents; they are not representative research or popularity evidence.
- [chess.js 1.4.0 documentation](https://jhlywa.github.io/chess.js/) and [source/license](https://github.com/jhlywa/chess.js) provide move legality, FEN/PGN and standard insufficient-material detection. Its threefold/50-move game-over shortcut must not determine our results. BSD-2-Clause, version pinned to npm 1.4.0 (upstream ce1ff9e9fc342984ff75ca475ab39f37888cb28a).
- [Stockfish.js maintainer](https://github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js/tree/v19.0.1): version 19.0.1, unmodified lite single-threaded worker, approximately 1.8 MB WASM. No shared-memory hosting headers required. GPL-3.0; source commit 0d5dd7944f88c817c07a26bc4fb1905d5f9c46d6. Corresponding-source bundle, networks, build instructions and licenses must be available alongside the binaries before publication.
- [FIDE 2023 rules, articles 5 and 9](https://handbook.fide.com/chapter/e012023): threefold/50-move claims (including an intended legal move), fivefold/75-move automatic draws; checkmate takes precedence. Use position identity including side, legal en passant and castling rights. Standard material draws are supported; exhaustive blocked-position deadness is not claimed.

## Visual review corrections

Integrated screenshots and measurements across all three engines exposed a stretched board at 320px/short landscape: shared 44px important minimums were overriding board-cell geometry. Chess now explicitly preserves square cells (at least 24px in supported layouts) while retaining 44px primary controls. Regression checks verify actual board and cell dimensions. The global palette toggle is mounted inside Game options and named High contrast, with an actual alternate board palette; it cannot float over Sound. Controls/notation use the reading font, last-move corners are darker, and 140ms piece/castling transitions honor reduced motion. Phone turn controls are compact and remain within the initial 320×568 view; healthy save text remains in other device-local guidance, while storage errors stay visible. Undo cannot reroll the computer's opening before the human has moved; loss/draw audio uses the appropriate cue rather than a victory jingle.

Integrated checkpoint review passed 15 viewport gameplays plus computer/hint/undo/continue/mute in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit; zero page errors, HTTP failures or music requests. Two axe scans have no violations, with contrast incomplete/manual review. Required existing mobile matrix passed 48+56 cases. ## Performance and cancellation refinement

A completed search now keeps one warm single-threaded worker for the next ordinary turn, avoiding repeated WASM transfer/compilation. Hash/search settings are reset per request. Undo, restart/mode changes, help/review, hidden/navigation and game completion still terminate active/idle workers; stale output remains rejected by request identity plus UI generation/position checks. Native regression verifies two ordinary computer turns use one WASM request and one worker. Fixture helpers explicitly dispose their separate analysis workers. Source cancellation tests remain meaningful rather than assuming worker construction proves safe cancellation.

A native hub assertion also exposed an asynchronous label race: initial Last played decoration preceded chess-save validation, leaving the old accessible label after visible Continue appeared. Shared decoration now updates labels it generated, preserving explicitly authored labels. The new browser assertion failed before this fix; failure logs are retained.

The first complete receiving-site gate passed: 4 documentation tests, 235 unit tests, 109 deploy fixture checks and 175 browser tests. This includes old games/content, actual withheld worker cancellation and offline/retry behavior. Final engine refinement/import and exact-head CI still pending.
